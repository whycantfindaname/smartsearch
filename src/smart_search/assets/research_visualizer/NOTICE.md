# Research Visualizer Attribution

This Smart Search Research Workspace visualizer is inspired by and adapted
from the Deep Research visualizer in
[`github/cafe3310/public-agent-skills`](https://github.com/cafe3310/public-agent-skills),
which is distributed under the MIT License.

The adaptation replaces the original generic task graph with the Smart Search
Root-led Stage G pipeline, removes third-party browser dependencies, and limits
the server to read-only projections of explicitly supported workspace files.

Copyright and license notices from the upstream MIT-licensed work remain
applicable to the portions adapted here.
